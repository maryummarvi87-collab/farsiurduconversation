Privacy Policy — Farsi Urdu Bol Chal

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Farsi Urdu Bol Chal («فارسی اُردو بول چال») is a Persian-language learning app for Urdu speakers, developed by Sayyada Maryam. This page explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. You do not need an account to use the app, and the app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only for the following purposes:

  • Content updates: the app downloads updated learning content (dictionary words, sentences, lessons) from the app's own content server. No personal information is sent with these requests.
  • «Ask AI» feature: when you type a question in the Ask AI section, the text of your question is sent to an external AI text service (Pollinations) so an answer can be generated and shown to you. Please do not type personal information into the Ask AI box. No identity or device information is attached to these questions by the app.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising and uses no analytics or user-tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

Learning content downloaded for offline use is stored only on your own device, inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

The app is an educational tool suitable for everyone and does not collect data from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
